Diet tracking, not medical advice

Dr. Natasha's GAPS App is a diet-tracking tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition, and it isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified practitioner. Symptom logging is there so you can see how your days line up with what you eat.

Questions

Good to know

What is Dr. Natasha's GAPS App?

A diet-tracking app for the GAPS Nutritional Protocol. It keeps your daily GAPS journal, shows each food's stage, offers recipes by stage and turns your journal into streaks and trends.

Who is it for?

Anyone following the GAPS Introduction Diet or Full GAPS, including parents following it with a child or the whole family.

Is it medical advice?

No. It's a diet-tracking tool. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Please speak to your own practitioner about medical questions.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, from the App Store (coming soon). Android, through early access on Google Play, with the public release to follow. And any phone or computer, in a web browser.

How do I get the Android app now?

Follow the three steps under “On Android” above: join the tester group, become a tester on Google Play, then install. Use the same Google account as on your phone, and please stay opted in for two weeks. That's what unlocks the public Android release.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: 14 days on the monthly plan on the App Store. The annual plan has no trial.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same account on your phone and in your browser.

Is it Dr. Natasha's official app?

Yes. It's Dr. Natasha's GAPS App, built around the GAPS Nutritional Protocol and her brand.
